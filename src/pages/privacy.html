Text you enter into the tools

Our tools (the Resume Bullet Generator, Email Generator, Social Media Caption Generator, Word Counter & Text Cleaner, and Product Description Generator) run as JavaScript in your browser. When you type or paste text, it is processed on your device. We do not send it to our servers or third parties, and we do not save it. When you close or reload the page, it is gone.

If you use the copy button, the text is placed on your device's clipboard, which is controlled by your operating system.

Data processed by our hosting provider

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver pages and protect the site against abuse, Cloudflare processes technical information about each request, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request. This is standard for websites. Cloudflare handles this data under its own privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

{{#if analytics}}

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand which pages are visited, so we can improve the site. It does not use cookies or local storage to track you and reports visits in aggregate. Learn more at cloudflare.com/web-analytics.

{{/if}}

Cookies and local storage

We do not set cookies.

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the name fk-theme, so the site remembers it on your next visit. It never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Our hosting provider may set strictly necessary security cookies, for example to tell legitimate visitors apart from automated attacks. These are not used for advertising.

If we add advertising or affiliate links in the future, those third parties may use cookies. We will update this policy first, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Emails you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to respond, and we delete correspondence when it is no longer needed.

Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites. Their privacy practices are their own, so please read their policies.

Children

The site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data about you, or to object to how it is used. Because we do not collect personal data through the tools, there is usually nothing to access. If you have emailed us, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the way the site works changes, for example if we add analytics, advertising, or a contact form. The date at the top shows the latest version.
