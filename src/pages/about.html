What we do

{{site.name}} is a small collection of free online tools for everyday work: writing resume bullet points, drafting professional emails, planning social media captions, counting and cleaning text, and writing product descriptions. Each tool does one job, works without an account, and gives you a result you can edit.

How the tools work

The current tools run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. The writing tools use rule-based templates written by people. They rephrase and arrange what you type, and they do not use a live AI model. We say this clearly on every tool, because you should know where your text comes from.

We also design the tools so they never invent facts. The resume tool will not make up achievements, and the product tool will not add claims about your product. Where a number or result would help, you get a [placeholder] to fill in yourself.

Our principles

  • Useful first. A tool should save you time on a real task, or it does not belong here.
  • No mandatory sign-up. You can use every tool without creating an account.
  • Privacy by default. What you type into the tools stays on your device.
  • Honesty. No fake reviews, invented statistics, or exaggerated claims about what the tools can do.

How the site is funded

The tools are free. To cover costs over time, we may add clearly labeled advertising, affiliate links to products we think are relevant, or optional paid templates. Any affiliate relationship will be disclosed. See our affiliate disclosure. The core tools will stay free to use.
