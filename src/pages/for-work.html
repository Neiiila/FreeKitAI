A simple workflow for job applications

  1. Update your experience. Use the Resume Bullet Generator one task at a time, then add real numbers where you have them.
  2. Check the length. Paste your cover letter into the Word Counter to stay within the employer's limit.
  3. Follow up. After an interview, draft a thank-you note with the Email Generator and send it while the conversation is fresh.
