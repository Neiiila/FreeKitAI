How to use the caption generator

  1. Describe the post in a few words, for example “our new autumn candle collection” or “tips for working from home”.
  2. Choose the platform. The layout, emoji, and hashtag count adjust to it.
  3. Add keywords you want people to notice and a call to action, then generate.
  4. Edit your favorite draft. The character count updates as you type.

How the hashtags are chosen

Hashtags are made only from your topic and keywords, for example “soy wax” becomes #SoyWax. The tool does not add trending or generic tags, because unrelated hashtags can attract the wrong audience. If you want more hashtags, add more specific keywords.

Limitations

  • The tool does not know your brand voice, current trends, or what performs well for your account. Treat the drafts as a starting point.
  • Template phrases can repeat if you use the same tone often. Edit them to sound like you.
  • It does not check whether a hashtag is already used for something unrelated. Search a hashtag before you rely on it.

Frequently asked questions

Are these captions generated by AI?

No. They are built from caption structures written in advance: a hook, an optional keyword line, your call to action, and hashtags. Your inputs fill in the details.

How many hashtags should I use?

There is no single correct number. A few specific, relevant hashtags are usually more useful than many generic ones. Platforms also publish their own advice, which changes over time.

Can I use these captions for client accounts?

Yes. The output is yours to use and edit. Review it for accuracy and brand fit first.
