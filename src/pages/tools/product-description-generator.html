This tool arranges your own facts into templates. It is not AI and does not add materials, certifications, health benefits, or performance claims. You are responsible for making sure your listing is accurate and follows the rules of your marketplace and country.

How to use the product description generator

  1. Enter the product name and a plain category, such as “ceramic mug” or “leather wallet”.
  2. List the features you can verify, one per line. These are the only facts the tool uses.
  3. Add your audience and a few search keywords, choose a tone, and generate.
  4. Copy each part into your store: the title, the short description for previews, the detailed description, and the bullets.

Why the tool only uses your facts

Product descriptions that overstate things lead to returns, bad reviews, and sometimes legal trouble. Claims about health, safety, certifications, or materials need proof. That is why the generator never invents them. If you enter a risky word such as “organic” or “cures”, it shows a reminder to check that you can back it up.

Example

You enter

Harvest Glow, scented candle. Features: hand-poured soy wax; burn time of about 40 hours; reusable glass jar. Audience: people who love a cozy home.

Short description (Professional)

Harvest Glow is a scented candle for people who love a cozy home. Key features include hand-poured soy wax and burn time of about 40 hours.

Limitations

  • The tool does not know your product. If a feature is vague, the description will be vague too, so be specific.
  • Features are used as you wrote them. Write them as short phrases such as “reusable glass jar” rather than full sentences.
  • Marketplaces have their own title and description rules. Check them before you publish.

Frequently asked questions

Does this use AI?

No. The generator fills structured templates with your product facts. It is predictable, fast, and free, and it cannot make up details about your product.

Can I use the text on Etsy, Shopify, Amazon, or my own site?

Yes, the text is yours to use. Each marketplace has its own listing rules, such as title length or banned claims, so check them before publishing.

Will this improve my search rankings?

We cannot promise that. Clear descriptions that use the words shoppers search for can help, but rankings depend on many factors outside your description.
