How to use the email generator

  1. Pick the purpose that is closest to your situation, such as “Follow up when you have had no reply”.
  2. Add a topic or reference so the subject line is specific, for example “Marketing Coordinator” or “Invoice #1042”.
  3. List the facts the reader needs, one per line. They are added to the email as you wrote them.
  4. Choose a tone and length, then select Write my email. Use Try another version for different wording.
  5. Fill in any [placeholders], read it once more, and paste it into your email app.

Choosing the right tone

  • Formal: first contact with a hiring manager, a professor, or a new client. No contractions.
  • Polite: a good default for most workplace emails.
  • Friendly: colleagues and people you already know. Uses contractions such as “I'm”.
  • Assertive: when you need a clear answer or action, such as an overdue payment or a second follow-up. It stays courteous but gets to the point.

Example uses

  • Thanking an interviewer within a day of the interview.
  • Asking a colleague for a 20-minute meeting next week.
  • Reminding a client that an invoice is due.
  • Asking a teacher for a deadline extension and giving a reason.

Limitations

  • The tool does not read your previous emails or know the person you are writing to. It cannot reply to a specific message for you.
  • Key points are inserted as you write them. They are not rewritten, so check their grammar.
  • For legal, medical, or financial matters, get advice from a qualified person before you send anything.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an AI email writer?

No. This first version uses a rule-based template engine: each purpose has hand-written sentence patterns, and your tone and length choices decide which ones are used. That keeps it fast, free, and private.

Are my emails saved?

No. The draft is created in your browser and is not sent to or stored on our servers.

How long should a professional email be?

As short as it can be while still giving the reader what they need. Use “Short” for quick replies and “Detailed” when the reader needs several facts to act.

When should I follow up if nobody replies?

It depends on the situation and on any timeline you were given. If you were told when to expect an answer, wait until that date has passed before following up.
