## Quality checklist for every article

- [ ] Answers the reader's question in the first two paragraphs.
- [ ] Has at least one original example, template, or table that isn't on every other site.
- [ ] Every factual claim (limits, statistics, policies) is checked against a primary source and dated.
- [ ] Links to the relevant tool where it genuinely helps, and to 1–2 related guides.
- [ ] Title under ~60 characters and meta description under ~155 characters (check with the Word Counter).
- [ ] Read on a phone before publishing.
